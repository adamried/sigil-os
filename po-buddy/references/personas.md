## Persona Selection Guide

When a feature touches multiple personas, the `/spec` skill surfaces a selection prompt. Use these rules of thumb:

- **Primary persona:** the one whose problem the feature most directly solves
- **Secondary personas:** affected by side effects but not the main beneficiary
- **Out of scope personas:** not affected — name them only if there's a risk of accidentally affecting them
