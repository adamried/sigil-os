# Planning and decision procedure

1. Inspect current architecture and existing conventions.
2. Identify the smallest file-level change set.
3. Define data flow, interfaces, migrations, error behavior, tests, rollout,
   and rollback.
4. For Enterprise or material unknowns, perform focused research with source
   dates and uncertainty.
5. Write a decision record for significant, difficult-to-reverse choices:
   context, options, decision, consequences, and status.
6. Validate that the plan satisfies the spec, design, constitution, and active
   waivers without speculative scope.
