# Specialist overlay merge protocol

Apply instruction layers in this fixed order:

1. base role constraints and safety rules;
2. selected specialist additions;
3. current task-specific instructions;
4. project constitution and active waivers.

Later layers may add or tighten constraints. A specialist or task layer may
not weaken base safety, approval, state-ownership, or constitution requirements.
Only an explicit, active waiver in layer 4 may permit a constitution exception;
no waiver can change Codex system, developer, sandbox, approval, or managed
policy.

Overlays are task-scoped and inert on their own. They do not become independent
agents. Selection is deterministic: normalize the task’s paths and keywords,
apply the table in `specialist-selection.md`, sort by the fixed category order,
and remove duplicates.
